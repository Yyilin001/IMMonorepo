## Pre-candidate regression policy

`IMV2/scripts/assess_v2_virtual_actor_capacity.py` records the integration owner's engineering budget, fixed before candidate sampling. After three stable main calibrations, `freeze --main-run ...` derives absolute limits from their medians and writes the immutable calibration artifact consumed by the runner. This is an explicit implementation acceptance budget, not a claim of user-approved production SLOs. Throughput and both recovery-query families must retain at least 90% of main; p95 may rise to the larger of 1.25× or +10 ms, p99 to the larger of 1.5× or +25 ms; SQL calls per accepted unit may rise to the larger of 1.35× or +2; server CPU per accepted unit may rise to 1.5×. Sampled Gateway PID-1 RSS may rise to the larger of 1.5× or +256 MiB, always below its 2 GiB container ceiling. Actor command permits remain hard bounded at 32,768 commands / 128 MiB, including queued, executing and reconciling work. These permits are a conservative upper bound, not a pure waiting-queue metric.

Accepted units are acknowledged messages for hot, same-owner, mixed and cold workloads, with mixed recovery query rates assessed separately. The query-only owner-fault workload uses successful room query items; retry/unavailable items are reported separately and never count as success. SQL counters include all captured DB statements, including observers and background workers. Server and loader CPU/RSS are separate; cgroup memory includes page cache and is not called process RSS. A missing batch metric is reported as unavailable, never as a zero-sized batch. Assessment writes every exceeded threshold; it cannot turn an incomplete run or missing required counter into a pass.
